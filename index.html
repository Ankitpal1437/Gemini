A recipe book that has never needed rewriting.

In 1961, Gemini Namkeen opened its doors in Ulhasnagar with a simple promise — fresh namkeen, made the same way every single day. Three generations later, that promise hasn't changed, even as the shop grew from a single counter to a name recognised across Ulhasnagar.

What started with a handful of namkeen varieties has grown into a full spread — Bikaneri bhujia, khatta meetha, laung sev, and premium dry fruits like cashew, pistachio and dry dates — all still weighed out, packed and sold the way it always was.
